General information
Enchant is positioned as a global music and sound creative group.
At a group level, we work with brands on a long-term basis to provide strategic oversight, creative craft and legal / financial support on their use of sound and music.
We believe that brands use sound in three ways; building equity, telling better stories and creating culture. The best brands do a mixture of all three.
Our goal is to become a music agency partner to the world’s biggest brands.
Enchant is comprised of three agencies: Sonicbrand, Resister and WMP.

Company one: Sonicbrand
Sonicbrand is the biggest agency in the group, and specialises in sonic branding, strategy audit and voice strategy.
All about beauty, experience and implementation.
Offices in London, Amsterdam, Riyadh and satellite offices in New York and LA.
Sonicbrand is the “original” sonic branding agency, established 20+ years ago when founder Dan Jackson wrote the foundational text on how to design sonic branding.
Sonicbrand is most relevant to brand design agencies, and people in brand-focused roles.

Company two: Resister
Resister is a women-founded and women-run music supervision agency, offering music licensing, music supervision, strategy and artist partnerships.
All about purpose, culture and creativity.
Founded by Hollie Hutton and Hannah Charman, mission is to re-balance the industry towards more diverse perspectives and voices in the music industry. 
Primary audience is creative agencies. Secondary audience is production companies, film studios and in-house creative agencies.

Company three: WMP
WMP is the smallest agency in the group, and specialises in custom composition; creating exceptional music from scratch. 
All about craft, simplicity and excellence.
Based in Leeds, UK.
Primary audience is music supervisors, secondary audience is experiential / events agencies, media and broadcast companies and creative agencies.

Below is an overview of our key services:

Sonic Branding
Sonic branding services are offered by Sonicbrand and Resister.
Sonic branding is creating a suite of sonic brand assets, such as a sonic logo, sonic DNA track or branded library. It is a complete system of owned sound.
Sonic branding is Sonicbrand’s flagship product, but Resister also offers simpler, smaller sonic branding projects.
We have completed sonic branding projects for Shell, Nescafe, Riyadh Air, Bacardi, Dolmio and Lucozade.
Sonic branding is well-suited to brands who have recently completed a brand refresh or rebrand, brands who are looking to grow, brands who advertise a lot, brands who have a consistency problem.
Sonic branding typically costs £50,000 to £120,000 including license depending on the scale of the project
Compared to MassiveMusic, Sonicbrand is more affordable and has better credentials for implementing and supporting long-term sonic identities.

Strategy Audit
Strategy audits are offered by Enchant and Sonicbrand.
We offer a comprehensive strategy audit, encompassing deep research into the brand and competitors
We have completed strategy audit projects for Nestle
This is well-suited to brands who are in the early stages of exploring how their brand should use sound, and are unsure of what the best approach should be. It is also a good fit for brands who already have an existing sonic identity
This is usually interesting for heads of marketing or heads of brand.
This typically costs between £20,000 to £40,000.

Custom Composition
Custom Composition is offered by Resister and WMP.
Custom Composition is writing and producing a bespoke soundtrack for an advertising campaign.
We have completed custom composition projects for CASS, This Girl Can, Papa John’s
Custom composition is well-suited to brands who do a lot of advertising, and are looking for music that will drive the narrative of the ad.
Custom composition is usually interesting for people who work in marketing or creative roles, or creative agencies
Custom Composition typically costs £15,000 to £30,000 depending on usage.

Music Supervision
Music Supervision is offered by Resister
Music supervision is finding and negotiating the rights for an existing piece of well-known music to be used on advertising or content.
We have completed music licensing projects for Barbie, Tesco, eBay, American Express.
Music supervision is well-suited to brands who do a lot of advertising.
Music surpervision is usually interesting for people who work in creative or marketing roles, or creative agencies.
Music Supervision typically costs 10% of the total negotiated cost of the license.

Product Sound
Product Sound is offered by Sonicbrand.
Product sound is creating sounds for physical and digital experiences such as apps, online interfaces, physical products, packaging and more.
Product sound is sometimes referred to as “UX / UI sound” in relation to apps and digital experiences.
We have completed product sound projects for Marantz, KitKat and Riyadh Air. 
Product is well-suited to brands who have an app, or create electronic goods and products
Product sound is usually interesting for marketing and product teams.
Product sound typically costs £15,000 to £40,000 and is usually done as part of a bigger sonic branding project.

Experiential Sound Design
Experiential sound design is offered by Sonicbrand and WMP.
Experiential sound design is creating sounds for retail, event or experiential environments. There are lots of ways to do this, ranging from simple playlisting all the way up to creating bespoke, generative soundscapes.
We have completed experiential sound design projects for Inverness Castle and Riyadh Air.
Experiential Sound Design is well-suited to brands who have physical spaces, such as retail brands, experience brands.
Experiential Sound Design is usually interesting for people who work in marketing.
Experiential Sound Design typically costs £20,000 up to £100,000 depending on which approach the brand takes.

Voice Strategy
Voice Strategy is offered by Sonicbrand.
Voice strategy is creating principles and guidelines for how brands select voice-over talent for advertising and content.
We have completed voice strategy projects for PwC.
Voice strategy is well-suited to brands who do a lot of TV and radio advertising, or B2B brands who do a lot of internal comms.
Voice strategy is usually interesting for people who work in marketing
Voice strategy typically costs £20,000 to £50,000

Music Curation / Playlisting
Music curation and playlisting is offered by Resister.
Music curation is finding and selecting music for playlists, retail, on-pack activations and more.
We have completed music curation projects for Bacardi.
Music curation is well-suited to brands who have retail, experiential, event or packaging touchpoints.

Research and Testing
Research and testing is offered by Sonicbrand and Resister.
We partner with the industry’s best testing partners to be able to test, validate and analyse the effectiveness of our work. 
Working with the likes of SoundOut, Veritonic or Cloud Army, can test and evaluate sonic branding, music licensing, composition projects and more.

Music Management
Music management is offered by Enchant Group.
Music management is providing complete oversight and support for a brand’s investment in music.
As part of a music management agreement, we provide agreed terms and conditions for music sourcing, a favourable, standardised rate card of costs, and quarterly business reviews on how the brand can optimise their music investment.
We do music management for Shell and DHL.
Music management is well-suited to larger brands that have large music-sourcing needs and put out a lot of advertising and content.
Music management is usually interesting for people who work in procurement or marketing roles.

Compliance Check
Compliance Check is offered by Enchant.
Compliance Check is a deep dive audit to check a brand’s social channels for any music that is a potential legal risk. We look for things like hit license music, TikTok CML and assign a risk score to music that should be changed, re-licensed or taken down. This provides greater legal safety and peace-of-mind.
We have completed compliance check projects for DHL.
Compliance Check is well-suited to large brands who do a lot of advertising and social content.
Compliance Check is usually interesting for people who work in procurement
Costs for Compliance Check vary depending on the number of channels that we analyse, number of posts we analyse and number of markets where we do the analysis.

“Listen” Music Guardianship Service
Music Guardianship is offered by Sonicbrand
“Listen” is the name of Sonicbrand’s music guardianship service, where sonic branding implementation is vetted and supported by our in-house team of experts.
The main brands who are using “Listen” is Shell and Nestle.
“Listen” is well-suited to brands who have large marketing teams and create a lot of advertising and content.
“Listen”  is usually interesting for people who work in marketing procurement or social media management.
Costs for “Listen” vary depending on the number of users, number of markets and the services they have access to.
